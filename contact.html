Frequently Asked Questions

How can I join EcoLearn as a student?

You can join EcoLearn as a student by signing up on our website. Simply go to the Sign Up page, select "Student" as your account type, and fill in your details. You'll need to provide your name, email, school name, and grade.

Can schools register with EcoLearn?

Yes, schools can register with EcoLearn. On the Sign Up page, select "School" as your account type and provide the required information including school name, address, official email, principal's name, and phone number.

What types of games are available on EcoLearn?

EcoLearn offers a variety of educational games including quizzes, simulations, puzzles, and action games. These games cover topics like climate change, water conservation, waste management, biodiversity, and renewable energy.

How can NGOs partner with EcoLearn?

NGOs can partner with EcoLearn by selecting "NGO" on the Sign Up page. You'll need to provide your organization name, focus area, email, address, and phone number. Our team will review your application and get in touch with you.

Is EcoLearn aligned with NEP 2020?

Yes, EcoLearn is fully aligned with India's National Education Policy (NEP) 2020. Our platform promotes experiential learning, skill development, and environmental awareness as outlined in the policy.
