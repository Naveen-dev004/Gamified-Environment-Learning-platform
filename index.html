The Problem

Why environmental education needs a transformation in India

Theory Without Practice

Environmental education in India is predominantly theoretical, with students memorizing facts from textbooks without practical application. This approach fails to instill genuine environmental consciousness or inspire actionable change.

According to recent studies, over 75% of Indian students cannot connect classroom environmental lessons with real-world applications, leading to a disconnect between knowledge and action.

UNESCO Research Finding

Gamified learning increases knowledge retention by 70% compared to traditional teaching methods.
